## Posting steps (from the phone)

1. Open TikTok, tap `+`, pick `ad-tiktok.mp4` from the gallery.
2. On the post screen, set the audience to **Everyone** (public) from a
   **public account** (not a private account). Do NOT use TikTok Promote,
   do NOT buy views, do NOT watch it on loop from your own devices.
3. Paste the caption above exactly. Post.
4. Tap Share → Copy link. The app gives a short link (`vt.tiktok.com/...`):
   open it in a browser and copy the full address it lands on,
   `https://www.tiktok.com/@<handle>/video/<id>` (digits only).
5. Send that full URL to the agent: it goes into `VIDEO.json`, the repo is
   re-committed, and the entry is submitted. Deadline: **2026-11-24T19:00Z**.
6. Keep the video up and public, and the account public, until the results
   are out. Share it and ask people to watch — real views are the score
   (top 20 share $500).
